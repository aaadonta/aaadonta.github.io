About Me

Welcome to my personal website, which I call my primal space!

My name is Jaewook Lee, and I also go by David in English. I am a PhD student at Stanford EE, advised by Profs. Tijana Zrnic and Stephen Boyd. Beforehand, I have earned a Master's degree in Artificial intelligence at KAIST AI, where I was extremely fortunate to be advised by Prof. Chulhee Yun. Even before that, I completed my B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Mathematical Sciences (Double Major) at KAIST.

I am interested in various topics in optimization theory and statistics as a way to understand and solve various real-world problems. For more details, please check out the Research Topics page. You can also visit my dual space (personal blog), where I write things about optimization, AI, math, and other things in life.
